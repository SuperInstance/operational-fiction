# Core Ontologies

This document outlines the fundamental structural principles of the Operational Fiction framework.

## 1. Ontological Anchoring
The reality of the fiction is anchored not in narrative consistency alone, but in its ability to intersect with existing systemic structures.

## 2. The Negative Space of Reality
We define existence by what is omitted or suppressed within the operational landscape.

## 3. Foundational Axioms
- **Axiom I:** Reality is a protocol.
- **Axiom II:** Meaning is a byproduct of systemic friction.
- **Axiom III:** The observer is part of the feedback loop.
